Spring Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out ideal spring arithmetic from the numbers you type. It treats a spring as a straight line from rate and compression. It does not know your real spring, where its rate stops being a straight line, how far it can travel before it bottoms out, how long it will last, or whether a part is safe to build with it, and it gives no verdict on any of that. Every number is your own entry.

The force truth

The push of a spring at a compression. Rate is in N/mm, compression in mm.

The energy truth

The energy stored at that compression, using the rate and compression above, in millijoules (mJ).

The travel truth

How far the spring above is compressed to push back with a force you pick.

The pair truth

The rate of two springs together, using the rate above and a second one.

Spring Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
